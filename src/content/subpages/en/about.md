Software developer based in San Luis Potosí, Mexico, with a degree in Intelligent Systems Engineering from the Universidad Autónoma de San Luis Potosí (UASLP).

It all started with a video about the prisoner's dilemma that hooked me, and I ended up going much deeper into the subject than I expected. First I read about Axelrod's tournaments, then about experiments on promises, and there I found the question behind this site: why we keep a promise that no longer pays. Working on it led me to write a working paper, build a simulation engine in TypeScript, and make this site.
